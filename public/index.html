The path to a real test

  1. Receive the operator bundle and confirm its release and network pins.
  2. Authorize a full GPU VM and verify SSH, Docker and container GPU access.
  3. Measure the exact Qwen checkpoint and BF16 backend identity.
  4. Benchmark inference and collect telemetry.
  5. Review TOPLOC, registration, calibration and accepted-work evidence under separate approval.

A successful inference request is one stage. It does not establish calibration or settlement.
